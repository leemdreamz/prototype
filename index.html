About

Independent. Uncompromising. Built for culture.

Prototype Pictures is an independent Los Angeles production company specializing in commercials, music videos, branded content, and narrative films. We partner with brands, agencies, artists, and studios to develop, produce, direct, and finish cinematic work from concept through final delivery. Every frame is built with intention, designed to move audiences and stand the test of time.

We build from the brief outward: shaping narratives, directing talent, and creating images that move people in the room and online. From commercial production to short-form storytelling, we work with brands and artists who want work with intent, sharp point of view, and staying power.
